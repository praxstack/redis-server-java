Sub-Agent and Multitask Behavior

Why you may see "no response" until a notification:

In multitask mode, the parent agent dispatches sub-agents (e.g., for parallel evidence gathering or HTML generation) and ends its turn immediately. Sub-agents complete asynchronously. The parent does not block waiting for all children. A notification arrives when the sub-agent finishes or when the coordinator aggregates results. This is expected behavior, not a hang or failure.
